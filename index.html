    FAQ

    Questions about the days until Christmas

    Which country celebrates Christmas first?

    Kiritimati (Christmas Island) in Kiribati is in the UTC+14 time zone, so it is one of the first inhabited places to reach midnight on December 25.

    Which place celebrates Christmas last?

    American Samoa, in the UTC−11 time zone, is among the last inhabited places to reach Christmas Day – about 25 hours after Kiritimati.

    How many weeks until Christmas?

    Divide the days left by seven. The countdown on this page shows the exact number of weeks, days, hours and minutes in real time.

    When are the 12 days of Christmas?

    In the Western tradition, the twelve days of Christmas run from Christmas Day, December 25, to January 5. January 6 is Epiphany.

    When is Orthodox Christmas?

    Many Orthodox churches that follow the Julian calendar celebrate Christmas on January 7.

    Is Christmas a public holiday everywhere?

    No. Christmas is a public holiday in most countries, but not in all – in Japan, for example, December 25 is a normal working day.
